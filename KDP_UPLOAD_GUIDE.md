# Amazon KDP Upload Guide: Step-by-Step Instructions

## Overview
This guide walks you through uploading your book to Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing (KDP) using the export package from Authors Bureau. The entire process takes 5-10 minutes once you have all your files ready.

---

## Before You Start

### What You'll Need
- [ ] Your KDP-ready manuscript (DOCX or PDF)
- [ ] Your book cover image (PNG or JPEG)
- [ ] Your optimized metadata (title, description, keywords, categories)
- [ ] ISBN (if using your own) or plan to use Amazon's free ISBN
- [ ] Copyright page content (optional but recommended)
- [ ] Amazon account (can use existing customer account)

### Account Setup
1. Go to [kdp.amazon.com](https://kdp.amazon.com)
2. Sign in with your Amazon account or create a new one
3. Complete your account information:
   - Legal name and address
   - Tax information (W-9 for US, W-8 for international)
   - Payment information (how you'll receive royalties)

**Time**: 5-10 minutes (one-time setup)

---

## Step 1: Create Your Book

### Navigate to Your Bookshelf
1. Log into [kdp.amazon.com](https://kdp.amazon.com)
2. Click **"Create"** in the top navigation
3. Choose your format:
   - **Kindle eBook** - Digital format for Kindle devices and apps
   - **Paperback** - Print-on-demand physical book
   - **Hardcover** - Premium print-on-demand option

**Tip**: You can create all three formats for the same book. Start with eBook (easiest), then add print formats later.

---

## Step 2: Enter Book Details (Metadata)

### 2.1 Language and Title
- **Primary Language**: Select the language your book is written in
- **Book Title**: Copy from your metadata file (max 200 characters)
- **Subtitle** (optional): Copy from your metadata file (max 200 characters)
- **Series** (optional): Enter series name and number if applicable

### 2.2 Edition and Author
- **Edition Number** (optional): Leave blank for first edition
- **Author**: Enter your name exactly as you want it to appear
  - Click "Add contributors" to add co-authors, editors, illustrators, etc.
- **Description**: Copy your optimized description from metadata file
  - **Character limit**: 4,000 characters
  - **HTML formatting**: You can use `<b>`, `<i>`, `<br/>`, `<ul>`, `<li>` tags
  - **First 3 lines are critical**: Visible before "Read more" button

### 2.3 Publishing Rights
- Select **"I own the copyright"** (if you wrote the book)
- Or **"This is a public domain work"** (if applicable)

### 2.4 Keywords
Enter your 7 optimized keywords (one per box):
- Use buyer-intent phrases, not single words
- Example: "personal finance for beginners" not just "finance"
- Max 50 characters per keyword

### 2.5 Categories
- Click **"Choose categories"**
- Select up to 2 categories during upload
- Choose the most specific subcategories possible
- Example: "Business & Money > Personal Finance > Budgeting" not just "Business"

**Tip**: You can request up to 10 categories total by contacting KDP support after publishing.

### 2.6 Age and Grade Range (if applicable)
- Only for children's books
- Select appropriate age range

---

## Step 3: Upload Your Manuscript

### 3.1 ISBN (Print Books Only)
Choose one option:
- **Amazon free ISBN**: Amazon listed as publisher, Amazon-only distribution
- **Your own ISBN**: Enter your ISBN-13 number (purchased from Bowker or your country's ISBN agency)

**Note**: eBooks don't need ISBNs - Amazon assigns a free ASIN automatically.

### 3.2 Publication Date
- **Leave blank** for "publish as soon as approved" (recommended)
- Or set a future date for pre-orders

### 3.3 Print Options (Print Books Only)
- **Interior Type**: Black & white or color
- **Paper Type**: White or cream (cream is warmer, more traditional)
- **Trim Size**: Most common is 6" x 9" for non-fiction, 5" x 8" for fiction
- **Bleed**: Choose "No Bleed" unless your design extends to page edges

### 3.4 Upload Manuscript File
1. Click **"Upload Manuscript"**
2. Select your DOCX file (eBook) or PDF file (print)
3. Wait for upload to complete (may take 1-5 minutes depending on file size)
4. KDP will automatically convert and validate your file

**Common Upload Errors**:
- **Track changes enabled**: Accept all changes in Word before exporting
- **Fonts not embedded**: Save PDF with embedded fonts
- **Images too large**: Compress images to under 5MB each
- **Incorrect trim size**: PDF must match selected trim size exactly

### 3.5 Preview Your Book
1. Click **"Launch Previewer"** after upload completes
2. Review your book on different devices (Kindle, tablet, phone)
3. Check for:
   - Formatting issues (spacing, indentation)
   - Page breaks in correct places
   - Images display correctly
   - Table of contents links work (if applicable)
4. If you find issues, fix your source file and re-upload

**Time**: 5-10 minutes for preview

---

## Step 4: Upload Your Cover

### 4.1 Cover Upload
1. Click **"Upload a cover you already have"**
2. Select your cover image file (PNG or JPEG)
3. **eBook requirements**:
   - Minimum 1000px on shortest side
   - Ideal: 2560 x 1600 pixels
   - Aspect ratio: 1:1.6 (height to width)
4. **Print requirements**:
   - Use KDP's Cover Calculator to get exact dimensions
   - Includes front, spine, and back cover
   - Must include 0.125" bleed on all sides

### 4.2 Cover Preview
- Review cover thumbnail
- Check that text is readable at small sizes
- Verify colors look correct

---

## Step 5: Set Your Pricing

### 5.1 Choose Territories
- **Worldwide rights**: Your book available in all Amazon marketplaces
- **Individual territories**: Select specific countries

### 5.2 Select Royalty Plan (eBooks)

**70% Royalty Option** (Recommended):
- **Price range**: $2.99 - $9.99
- **Earnings**: 70% of list price minus delivery costs (usually $0.06-$0.15)
- **Example**: $4.99 book = ~$3.40 per sale
- **Requirements**: Must be enrolled in all territories, book must meet quality standards

**35% Royalty Option**:
- **Price range**: $0.99 - $200
- **Earnings**: 35% of list price
- **Example**: $0.99 book = $0.35 per sale
- **Use when**: Price below $2.99 or above $9.99

### 5.3 Set Your Price
- **eBook**: $2.99 minimum for 70% royalty (recommended)
- **Paperback**: Minimum price based on printing costs (KDP calculates automatically)
- **Hardcover**: Higher minimum due to printing costs

**Pricing Tips**:
- Price eBooks at $2.99-$9.99 to maximize royalties
- Price paperbacks to earn $3-$5 profit per sale
- Check competitor pricing in your category
- You can change prices anytime after publishing

### 5.4 KDP Select (eBooks Only)
**KDP Select** = Exclusive to Amazon for 90 days

**Benefits**:
- Earn from Kindle Unlimited page reads (~$0.004 per page)
- Free promotion days (5 per 90-day period)
- Countdown deals
- Higher visibility in Amazon algorithms

**Drawback**:
- Cannot sell on other platforms (Apple Books, Google Play, etc.)

**Recommendation**: Try KDP Select for first 90 days, then decide if you want to go "wide" (other platforms).

## Step 6: Publish Your Book

### 6.1 Final Review
Review all information one last time:
- [ ] Title and subtitle correct
- [ ] Author name spelled correctly
- [ ] Description looks good (check for typos)
- [ ] Keywords entered
- [ ] Categories selected
- [ ] Manuscript uploaded and previewed
- [ ] Cover uploaded and looks good
- [ ] Pricing set correctly
- [ ] ISBN entered (if using your own)

### 6.2 Click "Publish Your Kindle eBook" (or Paperback/Hardcover)
- KDP will review your book (usually 24-72 hours)
- You'll receive an email when your book is live
- Book appears on Amazon.com and other marketplaces

### 6.3 Order a Proof Copy (Print Books)
**Highly Recommended**:
1. After publishing, order a proof copy at author cost (~$5-$10)
2. Review physical book for any issues
3. Check cover alignment, interior formatting, paper quality
4. Make corrections if needed and re-upload

## Step 7: After Publishing

### Monitor Your Book
1. **Bookshelf**: View all your published books
2. **Reports**: Track sales, royalties, and Kindle Unlimited page reads
3. **Marketing**: Access promotional tools and ads

### Update Your Book
You can update anytime:
- **Content updates**: Upload new manuscript file (typo fixes, content additions)
- **Cover updates**: Upload new cover image
- **Metadata updates**: Change description, keywords, categories
- **Pricing updates**: Adjust prices (takes effect within 24 hours)

**Note**: Content updates take 24-72 hours to review and go live.

### Expand to Other Formats
Once your eBook is live:
1. Create paperback version (uses same content, just needs print formatting)
2. Create hardcover version (premium option)
3. Create audiobook via ACX (Amazon's audiobook platform)

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### 1. Pricing Below $2.99 for eBooks
- **Mistake**: Pricing at $0.99 to attract buyers
- **Reality**: Earn only $0.35 per sale (35% royalty)
- **Better**: Price at $2.99+ to earn $2.09+ per sale (70% royalty)

### 2. Generic Keywords
- **Mistake**: Using single words like "business" or "romance"
- **Better**: Use specific phrases like "small business marketing strategies"

### 3. Broad Categories
- **Mistake**: Selecting top-level categories like "Business & Money"
- **Better**: Drill down to specific subcategories like "Business & Money > Small Business > Marketing"

### 4. Skipping the Previewer
- **Mistake**: Publishing without previewing
- **Reality**: Formatting issues are common and hurt reviews
- **Better**: Always preview on multiple devices before publishing

### 5. No Copyright Page
- **Mistake**: Forgetting to include copyright notice
- **Better**: Include copyright page at beginning of book (we provide this in your export package!)

### 6. Not Ordering Proof Copy (Print)
- **Mistake**: Assuming print book looks perfect
- **Reality**: Cover alignment, color, and formatting can surprise you
- **Better**: Always order proof copy before promoting

### 7. Ignoring Book Description
- **Mistake**: Writing boring, generic descriptions
- **Reality**: First 3 lines determine if readers click "Read more"
- **Better**: Use our AI-optimized descriptions with hook in first 3 lines

## Troubleshooting

### "Your book is under review"
- **Normal**: Takes 24-72 hours for first review
- **Longer**: If still under review after 72 hours, contact KDP support

### "Content quality issues detected"
- **Cause**: Formatting problems, low-quality images, or text issues
- **Fix**: Review error message, fix issues in source file, re-upload

### "Cover doesn't meet requirements"
- **Cause**: Wrong dimensions, low resolution, or text too close to edges
- **Fix**: Use KDP's cover templates or our cover generator

### "ISBN already in use"
- **Cause**: ISBN was used for another book or format
- **Fix**: Each format needs its own ISBN, or use Amazon's free ISBN

### Can't find my book on Amazon
- **Wait**: Takes 24-72 hours after "live" status
- **Search**: Search by exact title or ASIN (not ISBN)
- **Link**: Use direct link from your KDP bookshelf
