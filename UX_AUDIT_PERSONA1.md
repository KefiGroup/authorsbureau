## AMAZON KDP REQUIREMENTS RESEARCH COMPLETE

**Source:** AuthorImprints.com 20-Step Checklist + Amazon KDP Official Guidelines

### Critical Findings - What Authors Bureau Platform is MISSING:

#### 1. **ISBN Management** ❌ MISSING
- Platform doesn't mention ISBN at all
- No guidance on free vs paid ISBN
- No warning that ISBN decision is permanent
- No explanation that free Amazon ISBN locks you to Amazon only

#### 2. **Permanent Metadata Warning** ❌ MISSING  
- 9 metadata elements CANNOT be changed after publishing without losing reviews
- Platform doesn't warn users about this
- No confirmation dialog for permanent decisions

#### 3. **Legal Review Checklist** ❌ MISSING
- No prompts for trademarks, excerpts, music lyrics, unlicensed images
- No rights ownership verification
- Could cause 6-month delays (per AuthorImprints experience)

#### 4. **Format Specifications** ❌ MISSING
- No validation that files meet Amazon KDP specifications
- No trim size selection
- No paper type selection
- No barcode placement guidance

#### 5. **Complete Metadata Collection** ❌ PARTIALLY MISSING
- Platform collects: Title, description, categories, keywords ✅
- Platform MISSING: Subtitle, edition number, contributors, language, dimensions, paper type

#### 6. **eBook vs Paperback Decision** ❌ MISSING
- No guidance on format selection
- No explanation that eBook-only is faster
- No paperback-specific requirements

#### 7. **Editing Completion Verification** ❌ MISSING
- No confirmation that manuscript is final
- No warning about cost of changes after typesetting

#### 8. **Price Guidance** ❌ MISSING
- Platform shows "optimal pricing" in Ready to Publish workflow
- But no explanation of Amazon royalty tiers (35% vs 70%)
- No minimum/maximum price constraints

## Step 7: Ready to Publish Workflow - CRITICAL UX FAILURE

**What I See:**
- Page title: "AI-Powered Publishing"
- Subtitle: "Upload your manuscript and let our AI publisher optimize everything for Amazon KDP success"
- Progress bar with 4 steps: Upload → AI Analysis → Review & Edit → Publish
- Currently on step 1: "Upload"
- Section: "Upload Your Manuscript"
- Description: "Our AI will analyze your book like a New York Times publisher and optimize everything for Amazon KDP"
- Two tabs: "Paste Text" and "Upload File"
- Large textarea: "Paste your complete manuscript here..."
- Info box: "What happens next? Our AI will analyze your manuscript with the expertise of a senior New York Times publisher, suggesting bestseller-worthy titles, optimized descriptions, and market positioning strategies."
- Button: "Analyze with AI Publisher" (disabled - no manuscript uploaded yet)

**CRITICAL UX FAILURE #1: ASKING ME TO RE-UPLOAD MY MANUSCRIPT**

❌ **MAJOR PROBLEM:** I already uploaded my manuscript! It's in the system (13,201 words). Why is the platform asking me to upload it AGAIN?

**User Thought Process:**
"Wait... I just came from my book page where it showed my 13,201-word manuscript. Why do I need to paste or upload it again? Didn't the system save it? This makes no sense!"

**Expected Behavior:**
The "Ready to Publish" workflow should:
1. ✅ Detect that manuscript already exists in the system
2. ✅ Show preview: "Your manuscript: Hemispheric Intelligence (13,201 words)"
3. ✅ Skip directly to AI Analysis or show "Analyze Existing Manuscript" button
4. ✅ NOT ask for re-upload

**Amazon KDP Comparison:**
When you go to Amazon KDP with an existing manuscript file, you don't re-upload it multiple times. You upload once, then proceed through metadata, pricing, categories, etc.

**Impact:**
- Wastes user time
- Creates confusion and distrust ("Did my upload work?")
- Risk of uploading wrong version
- Breaks mental model of "my book is already in the system"

**Next Action:** I'll upload the manuscript again to continue the workflow (even though this shouldn't be necessary)
