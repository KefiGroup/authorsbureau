## Key Elements for All Back Covers:

### 1. Book Description Length
- 150-250 words optimal
- 3-5 short paragraphs
- Hook in first 2 sentences
- Benefits/outcomes clear
- Call to action implicit

### 2. Author Bio Length
- 50-100 words
- Credentials first
- Relevant experience
- Current work/position
- Optional: website/contact

### 3. Visual Hierarchy
- Title area (top 1/3)
- Description (middle 1/2)
- Author bio (bottom 1/4)
- ISBN/barcode (bottom right)

### 4. Optional Elements
- Endorsement quotes (1-2 max)
- Awards or recognition
- Series information
- Publisher logo
- QR code for bonus content

### 5. Typography Guidelines
- Title: Bold, 18-24pt
- Body text: 10-12pt
- Author bio: 9-11pt
- ISBN/category: 8-9pt
- Maintain 1.5 line spacing
